Connection to the AMX CLI was lost.

Studio runs locally — the page you were on is served by your CLI process. If the CLI was stopped (Ctrl-C, terminal closed, or a crash) the browser tab can no longer reach it.

To bring Studio back, open a terminal and run:

amx /studio

then refresh this tab. Your profiles and history are stored on disk — nothing was lost.
